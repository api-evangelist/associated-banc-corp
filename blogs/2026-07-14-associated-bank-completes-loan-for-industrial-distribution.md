CHICAGO – July 14, 2026 – Associated Bank recently announced completion of a loan to an affiliate of Molto Properties for construction of Molto Canal Pointe II. The property is a 210,748 square-foot Class-A industrial distribution facility 15 miles southeast of downtown Columbus, Ohio, at 7550 Robinett Way in Canal Winchester. Located at the northeast corner of Bowen Road and Robinett Way, the 23.67-acre parcel is currently vacant.
