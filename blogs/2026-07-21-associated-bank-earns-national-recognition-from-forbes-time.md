The honors include: Forbes World's Best Banks 2026 : recognizes banks that excel in customer trust, digital services, financial advice and overall customer satisfaction. Newsweek Readers' Choice Awards – Top 10 Best Regional Bank for 2026: celebrates organizations that inspire customer loyalty and consistently deliver meaningful value.
