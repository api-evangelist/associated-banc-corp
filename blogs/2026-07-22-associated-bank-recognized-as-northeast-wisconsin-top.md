GREEN BAY, Wis., July 22, 2026 – Associated Bank has been named a Northeast Wisconsin Top Workplace 2026 by Energage in partnership with the USA TODAY NETWORK-Wisconsin, recognizing organizations that foster exceptional, people-first workplace cultures. Associated was one of just 27 employers honored and is receiving the award for the second consecutive year. Employers are evaluated against organizations of similar size and ranked based solely on confidential employee feedback measuring key drivers of workplace engagement.
