GREEN BAY, Wis., July 28, 2026 – Associated Banc-Corp (NYSE: ASB) today announced Brianna Elsass has joined the company as executive vice president, chief digital officer. Reporting to Steve Zandpour, head of Consumer and Business Banking, Elsass will lead the bank's enterprise digital strategy, advancing innovative experiences that make banking simpler, more personalized and more seamless for customers.
