CHICAGO – July 27, 2026 – Associated Bank announced the completion of a $28,350,000 loan to a joint venture of North Park Ventures and SNS Realty Group for development of a new transit-oriented residential development at 3233-47 North Sheffield Avenue, Chicago. The loan included funds for acquisition of the former Tortenson Glass Co. which consists of nearly 30,000 square feet of urban infill land.
