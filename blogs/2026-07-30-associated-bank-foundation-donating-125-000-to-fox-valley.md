Green Bay, Wis., July 30, 2026 - Associated Banc-Corp (NYSE: ASB) today announced its foundation is donating $125,000 to support local recovery and relief efforts in response to the devastating storms that impacted Menasha and communities across the Fox Valley on July 27, 2026. As Wisconsin's largest bank holding company, Associated Bank has served communities across the state for more than 165 years. With colleagues, customers and their families living and working throughout Northeast Wisconsin, the bank is stepping forward to help communities begin the recovery process.
