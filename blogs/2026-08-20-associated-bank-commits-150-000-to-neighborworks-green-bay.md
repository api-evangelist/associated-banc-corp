GREEN BAY, Wis. — August 20, 2026 — Associated Banc-Corp (NYSE: ASB ) ("Associated") today announced a three-year, $150,000 grant commitment to NeighborWorks Green Bay to expand affordable and sustainable housing, homeownership education and workforce development across the Green Bay region. The grant provides $50,000 annually from 2026 through 2028.
