CHICAGO – August 24, 2026 – Associated Bank announced the completion of a $9 million loan to GTZ Properties for development of Trailhead Crossing, a multi-building retail project at 1135-1131 US-36 in Delaware, Ohio, a suburb of Columbus. The twelve-acre project will consist of three freestanding buildings occupied by Chik-Fil-A, Chipotle Mexican Grill and Take 5 Oil Change; a second three-tenant strip will be occupied by Dunkin Donuts, Nothing Bundt Cakes and Pacific Dental Services. Of two pad-ready sites, one is already pending sale to a national daycare company.
