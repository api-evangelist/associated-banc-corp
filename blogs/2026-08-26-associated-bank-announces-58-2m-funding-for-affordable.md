DALLAS – August 26, 2026 – Associated Bank, as lead arranger and administrative agent, announced completion of a $58,220,000 loan to Dominium, a national developer, owner and manager of affordable housing, for construction of The Brooks at Rose Hill at 1200 Rose Hill Road in Terrell, Texas. The 4-percent LIHTC project will be available to residents with incomes that do not exceed 60 percent of the area median income. Great Southern Bank was a direct participant in the financing package.
