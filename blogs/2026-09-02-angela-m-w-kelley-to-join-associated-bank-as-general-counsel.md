GREEN BAY, Wis., September 2, 2026 — Associated Banc-Corp (NYSE: ASB) (“Associated”) today announced that Angela M.W. Kelley, 45, will succeed Randall J. Erickson, 67, who is retiring as executive vice president, general counsel and corporate secretary effective October 13, 2026.
