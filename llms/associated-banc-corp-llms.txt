# Associated Bank

> Associated Bank, N.A. is the primary banking subsidiary of Associated Banc-Corp (NYSE: ASB), a nationally chartered commercial bank and the largest bank holding company headquartered in Wisconsin (Green Bay). With roughly $50 billion in total assets, it operates a full-service Midwest franchise across Wisconsin, Illinois, Iowa, Minnesota, Missouri and Nebraska. Associated does not publish a first-party public developer portal or downloadable OpenAPI/Swagger specifications; core-banking integration is mediated by its core provider Jack Henry (jXchange SOAP), and consumer-permissioned data access is delivered through third-party aggregators (Plaid, Tink/TrueLayer).

## APIs

- [Associated Bank jXchange (Jack Henry) Integration](https://jackhenry.dev/jxchange-soap/api-provider/associated-bank/): Partner/enterprise-gated core-banking SOAP surface via Jack Henry jXchange. Documented services for this institution include Account History Search, Account Inquiry, Account Relationship Search and Transaction Add. No public WSDL/OpenAPI is published by Associated Bank itself.
